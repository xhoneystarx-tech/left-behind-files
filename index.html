      Bring the light home

      Fire a laser at the Moon. Build the mirror that sends it back.

      Five steps. One big puzzle.
      Earth and Moon sizes are to scale. The gap between them is not: 30 Earths would fit in it.

      This quest needs 3D graphics, which this browser could not start. The facts are below.

      What scientists learned
      • Each mirror is a panel of glass corners. Apollo 11 and Apollo 14 left 100 each. Apollo 15 left 300.
      • A corner of three mirrors sends light straight back where it came from, even when the Moon wobbles.
      • The beam leaves Earth a few meters wide and is about 2 km wide when it reaches the Moon.
      • The echo takes about 2.5 seconds. Timing it gives the distance to within a few millimeters.
      • The Moon drifts 3.8 cm farther from Earth every year. That is about how fast your fingernails grow.
      • In 2025 the Blue Ghost lander delivered a new NASA mirror, and it has already returned laser light.

      Mars

      Every resident is a robot. Be three of them.

      Two rovers. One helicopter. Bring Mars home.
      Perseverance, at work in Jezero Crater since 2021. Model: NASA 3D Resources.

      This quest needs 3D graphics, which this browser could not start. The facts are below.

      What scientists learned
      • Perseverance carries 43 sample tubes. By July 2025 it had used 33 of them. Ten wait on the ground at a spot called Three Forks.
      • Each rock core is about the size of a piece of classroom chalk: 13 mm wide and 60 mm long.
      • One core, named Sapphire Canyon, has spots that might be a sign of ancient life. Only labs on Earth can tell.
      • NASA and Europe designed a lander, a small rocket and an orbiter to fetch the tubes. It cost too much and was cancelled. In 2027 a new NASA team starts looking for another way.
      • Curiosity's first drill hole, in 2013, was 1.6 cm wide and 6.4 cm deep. The powder was grey, not red.
      • Curiosity's wheel tracks spell J-P-L in Morse code. The rover uses the marks to check how far it has rolled.
      • A radio signal takes 3 to 22 minutes to cross from Mars to Earth. It depends on where the two planets are.
      • Ingenuity flew by itself. Nobody can steer with a joystick when the signal takes minutes to arrive.

          About the team and the challenge
          Project
          The Left Behind Files
          Challenge
          NASA Space Apps Challenge: tell the story of the hardware NASA has left across the solar system, for school-age space enthusiasts.
          Team
          Quantumers. Kien Do, Vietnam (team owner). Making space easy and fun for kids.
          The problem
          Sixty years of machines sit on the Moon and Mars, and most students have never heard what they are, where they are, or what they taught us.
          Our solution
          A detective story with real NASA coordinates on 3D globes, NASA's 3D spacecraft models, a laser experiment, and Hy, an animated hydrogen atom guide who answers questions with AI.
          How it was made
          We are hoping to make learning about space easy and fun for children. This website was built with the help of Claude.
